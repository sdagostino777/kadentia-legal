Medical disclaimer

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This app is not a medical device

It does not provide medical advice. Always consult your doctor before changing your therapy.

What that means in practice

The app contains no titration schedules and no reference doses, and it does not calculate doses: you enter the dose your doctor prescribed. The estimates it shows (level trend, weight projections) are built from the data you enter and take no account of your clinical history, contraindications, allergies, interactions with other medications or your test results.

The estimates the app shows — the level trend derived from the doses you logged, the projected date you will reach your target weight, the correlation between symptoms and dose — are mathematical elaborations built on the data you enter. They are meant to help you observe trends and talk to your doctor more effectively. They are not measurements, they are not diagnoses, and they cannot replace a clinical examination.

The decisions remain yours and your doctor's

The decision to start, increase, reduce, pause or stop a therapy belongs to you together with your treating physician. The app offers a tool to record what happens and to bring organised data to the conversation: nothing more.

When not to wait

Contact your doctor or emergency services if you experience:

  • severe and persistent abdominal pain, especially with vomiting
  • continuous vomiting or inability to keep fluids down
  • signs of dehydration: very dry mouth, dark urine, dizziness, sudden weakness
  • jaundice, meaning yellowing of the skin or eyes
  • fast resting heart rate or palpitations
  • breathing difficulty, swelling of the face or lips, widespread skin rash
  • any symptom that frightens you or that you have never experienced before

If you log a high-severity side effect, the app flags it and suggests talking to your doctor. That is a reminder, not a clinical assessment: if you feel unwell, call for help.

About products bought online

Some molecules discussed for weight loss, such as retatrutide, are not approved by any regulatory agency and are not available from pharmacies. The app includes them because some people are using them anyway within clinical trials or buying them privately, and in that case logging doses and effects is still useful.

The app cannot verify what an online purchase contains, cannot guarantee that a substance is genuine, and cannot advise you to use it. If you are considering that route, speak to a doctor first.

About weight and numbers

The journey with these medications is not linear. Weight fluctuates with water retention, menstrual cycle, salt, sleep and stress. One day that goes up does not erase a month of work, and a projection calculated from two weeks is an estimate, not a promise.

In an emergency

In the European Union call 112. In the United States call 911. Elsewhere, use your local emergency number. The app is not designed to handle emergencies and does not send alerts to anyone.
