Support

For problems, questions, bug reports or requests about your data, write to dagostino.simone@icloud.com. A person answers, not an automated system.

If your question is about a purchase or a subscription, you can also manage it from your own account: Apple's instructions for cancelling a subscription.

In short

Kadentia has no server. Everything you enter — weight, injections, doses, measurements, side effects and photos — stays on your device and is never sent to anyone, not even to us. We do not collect your name, your email, your location or any gender data.
