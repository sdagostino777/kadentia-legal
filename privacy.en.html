Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

The short answer

Kadentia has no server. Everything you enter stays on your device, in a local database protected by iOS encryption. We do not collect your name for identification, your email, your location, your contacts, or any gender data. We use no analytics, show no advertising, and share nothing with third parties.

This page explains how that works in detail, because transparency about health data is not optional.

1. Who processes the data

The data controller is Simone D'Agostino, Calle Ollerías 20, 29012 Málaga, Spain, reachable at dagostino.simone@icloud.com.

For the data the app stores on your device, the controller has no actual access: there is no technical channel through which it could read them. This policy still describes them, because you have the right to know what the app you use is doing.

3. Where the data stays

On your device, in two places:

  • The app's local database (SwiftData), inside the app's private folder.
  • A shared folder for the widget extension, used only to show the countdown to your next injection on the Lock Screen. It holds no identifying data.

Photos are stored as separate files inside the same protected folder. The operating system encrypts this data while the device is locked.

There is no backup on our systems, because we have no systems. If you enable iCloud backup on your device, that backup follows whatever rules you chose for iCloud: it is an Apple feature, not an app feature.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment data: card details, billing address and buyer identity stay between you and Apple. The app receives from StoreKit only the confirmation that a subscription is active, its expiry date and the plan type. That information lives on the device and is used to decide which features to show you.

To exercise rights over purchase data (refunds, invoices, history), the reference is Apple Support.

6. Transfers and recipients

We transfer data to no one, for no purpose, in no country: the data simply does not leave the device. The app contains no third-party SDKs, contacts no advertising servers, uses no tracking identifiers, and makes no network requests.

There is one exception, and you trigger it explicitly: when you use Export data, the app creates a JSON or CSV file and opens the system share sheet. From that moment the destination is your choice, and the app keeps no record of it.

7. How long data is kept

As long as you decide. Data stays on the device until you delete it from the app's settings ("Delete all data") or uninstall the app. Uninstalling makes iOS remove the app's private folder and all its contents.

The app keeps no copies on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Your rights

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you the right to access your data, correct it, erase it, restrict processing, object, and receive it in a portable format. Because the app keeps everything on your device, these rights are exercised directly inside the app:

  • Access: your data is visible across the app's screens.
  • Correction: you can edit every entry from settings or the log.
  • Erasure: "Delete all data" in settings removes everything permanently.
  • Portability: "Export data" produces a complete JSON file and a CSV of injections.
  • Restriction or objection: turn off any system permission you do not want to grant.

If you believe the processing does not comply with the law, you may contact the supervisory authority in your country.

9. Children

The app is not intended for people under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from minors. If you use it for a minor, responsibility for consent lies with the person holding parental responsibility.

10. Health data: a necessary clarification

The data you enter concerns your health and belongs to the category of special-category data, which the GDPR protects with stricter rules. The legal basis for processing is your explicit consent, given when you accept this policy and when you grant each individual permission. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your data or uninstalling the app.

The app is not a medical device, does not diagnose and does not provide medical advice. The calculations it shows (estimated drug level, weight projections, correlations between symptoms and dose) are educational estimates built from the data you enter, designed to help you discuss your therapy more effectively with your doctor.

11. Security

Data is protected by device encryption and, if you enable it, by Face ID locking when the app opens. Since nothing is transmitted, nothing can be intercepted. We still recommend protecting your phone with a passcode: it is the first line of defence for your health data.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we will say so inside the app and update the date at the top of this page. We will never reduce the protections described here without asking for your explicit consent again.
