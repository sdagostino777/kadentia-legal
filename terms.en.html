2. What the app does, and what it does not do

The app lets you log injections, doses, weight, body measurements and side effects; receive reminders; consult charts and estimates; export your data and generate a summary to show your doctor.

The app is not a medical device, does not diagnose, does not prescribe medication and does not provide medical advice. The titration schedules it reports reproduce public information from the official product leaflets and take no account of your clinical history, contraindications, interactions with other medications or test results. The calculations we show — estimated drug level, weight projections, correlations between symptoms and dose — are estimates built from the data you enter and may differ from clinical reality.

Do not change your therapy based on what the app shows without first speaking to a doctor.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Enter accurate data: the estimates depend on it.
  • Decide with your doctor whether and how to change dose and interval.
  • Protect your device with a passcode and, if you wish, enable Face ID locking in the app: your health data lives there.
  • Keep your own copies of exported data: the app keeps no copy anywhere else.
